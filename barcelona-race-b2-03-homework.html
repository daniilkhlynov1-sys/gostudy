Task 01 · 6 min

Words in context

Complete each sentence with a word or phrase from the box. Use each expression once.

look into · a mix-up · jumped to conclusions · backed up her claim · set the record straight · fair play

1. The registration desk made a mistake. There was _______.
2. The photographs _______ and proved that her bib had been switched.
3. The organisers promised to _______ the strange result.
4. Leila almost _______ when she accused another runner in her thoughts.
5. After checking the records, the organisers could _______.
6. Helping a rival runner was a great example of _______.
Task 02 · 5 min

A second message from Spain

Read this fictional follow-up email from the organisers and answer the questions.

Subject: An update on your race result

Dear Leila,

Thank you for reporting the error calmly. We have corrected both athletes' times. The updated certificates are now available. The photos we checked were taken by the official event photographer, and both runners have confirmed their bib numbers.

We have also reviewed the registration desk. From next year, volunteers will ask athletes to say their full name and date of birth before collecting their envelope. We hope this will prevent a similar mix-up.

We would be happy to welcome you to another race in Barcelona.

Best wishes,
Clara · Race team

1. Why does Clara thank Leila?
2. Which source helped confirm the runners' identities?
3. What is the organisers' preventive action?
Task 03 · 5 min

Past deductions and regrets

Choose the grammatically correct and contextually suitable sentence.

1. The volunteer says the envelopes were switched. The envelopes _______ by mistake.
2. Leila regrets not looking at the envelope. She _______ the surname.
3. If she had posted that accusation, she _______ the other runner's reputation.
4. The chip data was accurate. The system _______ completely.
Task 04 · 8 min

Write to the race organisers

Imagine you are Leila. Write a 120–150 word email after receiving the corrected result. Your tone should be polite but constructive.

  1. Thank the organisers for investigating.
  2. Explain the problem and what might have caused it.
  3. Suggest two improvements to registration.
  4. Reflect on fair play and your own first reaction.
Try to use: look into, a mix-up, set the record straight, should have, might have.
